FitFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

FitFlow ("the app") is provided by Kavidu Purnamal ("we"). This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and your choices. Contact: fitflow.support@gmail.com

Data we collect

Name, email and password (handled by Firebase Authentication); age, height, weight, goal and diet; logged workouts, steps, water, sleep and meals; and photos and posts you choose to share.

How we use it

To run your account, personalise plans, show your progress and operate the community feed. We never sell your data or use it for advertising or tracking.

AI processing

Your goal, fitness level, diet and body details (not your name or email) are sent to Google Gemini only to generate workout and meal plans. AI plans are suggestions and may contain mistakes.

Social features

Posts, photos and likes are visible to other FitFlow members. You can delete your own posts.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), and the AI provider acts as a service provider. Data is encrypted in transit. It may be processed outside your country under standard contractual clauses.

Your rights (GDPR)

Our legal basis is contract (to provide the app) and your consent (health data and photos). You may access, correct, delete, export or restrict your data, withdraw consent at any time, or complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, email fitflow.support@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days, and data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion.

Health data and HIPAA

FitFlow is a wellness app, not a healthcare provider or insurer, so HIPAA does not apply to it. We still treat health data as sensitive: we collect only what is needed, never sell it, and share it only as described in this policy.

Children

FitFlow is intended for people aged 18 and over.

Security and changes

We protect data with access rules and encryption in transit, but no system is completely secure. We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes.

Medical disclaimer

FitFlow provides general fitness and nutrition information and is not medical advice. Consult a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet programme.

Contact
